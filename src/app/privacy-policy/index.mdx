# Privacy Policy — NovaDirect Software

**Last Updated: August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how NovaDirect Software ("NovaDirect," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you visit novadirectsoftware.com (the "Website") or interact with us through our contact forms, discovery call bookings, or other communications. By using our Website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Who We Are

NovaDirect Software is a product of Corivance Solutions Private Limited, a company incorporated in India. We provide white-label direct selling and commission management software to businesses globally.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us at:
- **Email:** info@novadirectsoftware.com
- **Website:** novadirectsoftware.com

---

### 2. What Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following ways:

#### Information You Provide Directly
When you fill out our contact form or book a discovery call, we may collect:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your company name
- Any information you voluntarily include in your message or meeting notes

#### Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our Website, we automatically collect certain technical information including:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Referring website or source
- Device type and operating system

This information is collected through cookies and analytics tools. Please see Section 6 for more information about cookies.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:
- To respond to your enquiries and messages
- To schedule and conduct discovery calls
- To understand how visitors use our Website and improve the experience
- To send follow-up communications related to your enquiry, where you have consented
- To comply with our legal obligations

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

---

### 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds:
- **Consent** — where you have voluntarily submitted your information through our contact or booking forms
- **Legitimate Interests** — for website analytics and improving our services
- **Legal Obligation** — where we are required to process data to comply with applicable laws

### 5. Third Party Tools and Services

We use the following third party tools that may process your personal data:

#### Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and visitor behaviour. Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

#### Calendar and Booking Tools
We use a third party scheduling tool to manage discovery call bookings. When you book a call, the tool collects your name, email address, and any notes you provide.

#### Email Communication Tools
We may use an email platform to manage and respond to enquiries. Your email address and communication history may be stored within these tools.

All third party tools we use are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

---

### 6. Cookies

Our Website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and collect analytics data.

**What are cookies?**
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us understand how visitors interact with our Website.

**Types of cookies we use:**
- **Essential cookies** — necessary for the Website to function correctly
- **Analytics cookies** — help us understand visitor behaviour and improve the Website

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Website.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy.
- Enquiry and contact data is retained for a period of 24 months from the date of your last interaction with us, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
- Analytics data is retained in accordance with the retention settings of the relevant tools.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We may share your data with:

### 10. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data.

**For all users (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023):**
- The right to access the personal data we hold about you
- The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- The right to request erasure of your personal data
- The right to withdraw consent at any time

**For users in the European Union (under GDPR):**
- The right to access your personal data
- The right to rectification of inaccurate data
- The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- The right to restriction of processing
- The right to data portability
- The right to object to processing
- The right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

**For users in California, USA (under CCPA):**
- The right to know what personal data we collect and how it is used
- The right to request deletion of your personal data
- The right to opt out of the sale of personal data (we do not sell personal data)
- The right not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@novadirectsoftware.com. We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law.
