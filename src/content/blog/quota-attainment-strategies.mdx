Quota attainment is the metric everyone watches and few teams engineer deliberately. When attainment drops below 60% org-wide, the issue is rarely individual effort — it's territory, ramp, or comp design.

## Diagnose before you adjust

Pull these cuts before changing quotas:

- **Attainment by tenure** — are new reps the drag, or is everyone struggling?
- **Attainment by territory** — are some patches structurally under-resourced?
- **Attainment by segment** — enterprise vs. mid-market vs. SMB
- **Pipeline coverage at start of quarter** — was the number achievable on paper?

## Territory design principles

### Balance opportunity, not just accounts

A territory with 200 accounts but no qualified pipeline is a quota trap. Weight territories by:

- Historical win rate in the patch
- Addressable market size
- Existing customer expansion potential
- Inbound lead volume by region

### Refresh annually, adjust quarterly

Markets shift. Reps who crushed it in a hot territory shouldn't carry inflated quotas when the patch cools.

## Ramp quotas that set reps up to win

| Month | Quota as % of full |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| 1–2 | 0% (learning) |
| 3 | 25% |
| 4 | 50% |
| 5 | 75% |
| 6+ | 100% |

Pair ramp quotas with ramp commissions — reps should earn meaningfully during ramp, not just survive until month six.

## Comp plan alignment

Common misalignments that kill attainment:

- **Quota too high for coverage** — 3x pipeline coverage sounds healthy until win rates drop
- **Comp accelerators unreachable** — if nobody hits 100%, accelerators demotivate instead of inspire
- **SPIFFs that distract** — short-term promos that pull reps off core quota pursuits
- **Clawbacks that create fear** — reps sandbag instead of pushing deals across the line

## Manager levers

Quotas are set at the top, but attainment is managed weekly:

- **Pipeline reviews** focused on deal quality, not just quantity
- **Early warning system** — flag reps below 40% attainment at mid-quarter
- **Coaching investment** — top performers shadowed, not just celebrated
- **Remove blockers** — pricing approval, legal review, and SE availability

## Conclusion

Sustainable quota attainment comes from territories with real opportunity, ramp curves that respect learning time, and comp plans that reward the behavior you want. Fix the system, and individual performance follows.
