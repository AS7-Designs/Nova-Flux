RevOps teams shouldn't spend their week on manual data fixes. These five automations recover roughly 10 hours per week for a 20-person sales org — and they take less than a day each to configure.

## 1. Auto-assign leads by territory (2 hrs/week saved)

**Trigger:** New lead created
**Logic:** Match state/country/employee count to territory rules
**Action:** Assign owner, set SLA task, notify via Slack

No more round-robin spreadsheets or leads sitting unassigned over weekends.

## 2. Stale opportunity alerts (2 hrs/week saved)

**Trigger:** No activity on open opportunity for 14 days
**Action:** Create follow-up task, notify manager, add "At Risk" tag

Managers stop manually scanning pipeline for neglected deals.

## 3. Stage advancement gates (2 hrs/week saved)

**Trigger:** Rep attempts to move deal to next stage
**Logic:** Validate required fields are populated
**Action:** Block move with clear error message listing missing fields

Forecast accuracy improves because stage changes require evidence.

## 4. Closed-won handoff to CS (2 hrs/week saved)

**Trigger:** Opportunity marked Closed Won
**Action:**

- Create CS onboarding project with deal context
- Copy key contacts and contract details
- Schedule internal handoff meeting
- Send welcome sequence trigger to CS platform

Sales stops Slack-threading account details. CS starts with full context.

## 5. Weekly pipeline summary to leadership (2 hrs/week saved)

**Trigger:** Every Monday 7 AM
**Action:** Generate and email report with:

- Pipeline created vs. closed last week
- Top 5 deals by amount with risk flags
- Rep activity leaderboard
- Forecast vs. target gap

Leaders get consistent data without RevOps building slides.

## Implementation tips

- **Start with one automation** — prove value before stacking five
- **Document every rule** — future you (and new RevOps hires) will thank you
- **Monitor error logs** — automations fail silently if nobody watches
- **Get rep buy-in** — explain what each automation does and why

## Conclusion

The best RevOps automations are boring: assign, alert, validate, handoff, report. Boring saves hours — and gives your team time for the strategic work that actually moves revenue.
