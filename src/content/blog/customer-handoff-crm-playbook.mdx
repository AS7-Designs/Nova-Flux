The worst churn happens in the first 90 days — often because CS inherits an account with no context on why the customer bought, who the champion is, or what was promised during sales.

## The handoff failure modes

- **The blank slate** — CS gets a closed-won notification and nothing else
- **The Slack thread** — critical context buried in DMs, never in CRM
- **The over-promise** — sales commitments not documented on the opportunity
- **The wrong contacts** — billing contact onboarded instead of the champion

## The CRM-native handoff playbook

### Required fields at Closed Won

Block the stage change until these are populated:

- **Primary champion** — name, role, and why they bought
- **Success criteria** — what does "value realized" look like in 90 days?
- **Implementation timeline** — agreed go-live date and milestones
- **Risks flagged** — competitive threats, budget concerns, stakeholder gaps
- **Promised deliverables** — anything custom discussed in late-stage calls

![Team collaborating in an open workspace](/images/blog/compagnons-Fa9b57hffnM-unsplash.webp)

### Automated handoff workflow

**On Closed Won:**

1. Create CS onboarding project with all opportunity fields mapped
2. Assign CS owner based on segment/territory rules
3. Schedule internal handoff call (30 min, within 48 hours)
4. Generate customer-facing welcome email from template
5. Notify sales rep: "Handoff initiated — CS owner assigned"

### The internal handoff call agenda

Keep it to 30 minutes with a standard template:

1. **Why they bought** (5 min) — in the champion's words
2. **Stakeholder map** (5 min) — who's engaged, who's missing
3. **Risks and landmines** (5 min) — what could derail onboarding
4. **Timeline and expectations** (5 min) — what was promised and when
5. **Q&A** (10 min) — CS asks, sales answers

Record it. Link the recording to the account in CRM.

## Measure handoff quality

| Metric | Target |
| ------ | ------ |
| Handoff call completed within 48 hrs | 95%+ |
| Required fields populated at close | 100% |
| 90-day churn on new accounts | Track vs. baseline |
| CS satisfaction with handoff context | Quarterly survey |

## Conclusion

Great handoffs aren't about more meetings — they're about the right data in CRM at the moment of close. Automate the workflow, enforce the fields, and protect the customer relationship you worked hard to win.
