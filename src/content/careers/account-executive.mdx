Flux is hiring an Account Executive to bring our AI CRM to revenue teams who are ready to move faster than their current stack allows.

## About the role

You'll own the full sales cycle for mid-market accounts — from first conversation to closed-won. Flux sells itself when reps see AI-drafted follow-ups and pipeline automations in action, so you'll spend less time on slides and more time running demos that make deals feel inevitable.

## What you'll do

- Prospect and qualify inbound and outbound opportunities
- Run discovery and product demos tailored to each team's workflow
- Build multi-threaded relationships with sales leaders, RevOps, and executives
- Partner with customer success to ensure smooth handoffs and expansion
- Provide feedback to product on what prospects need to hear yes

## What we're looking for

- 3+ years closing B2B SaaS deals, ideally in sales tooling or CRM
- Track record of hitting or exceeding quota
- Clear, concise communication — you can explain AI value without the jargon
- Curiosity about how modern revenue teams operate
- Bonus: experience selling to sales teams (you've felt the CRM pain firsthand)

## Why Flux

We built Flux because we lived the pipeline grind. You'll be selling a product our team actually uses — with stories, demos, and proof points that come from real revenue experience.
